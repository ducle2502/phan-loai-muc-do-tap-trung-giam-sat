# Classification rules

## Minimum facts

Assess objective/output, timing expectation, current result, evidence or testable completion claim, accountable PIC/unit, and material consequence/dependency/decision. Not every field must exist. Use `XÁM` only when gaps prevent reliable assessment.

## ĐỎ

Requires both:

1. evidenced serious consequence or failed mandatory readiness, such as serious effect on operational safety, law, liquidity, capital, critical data, or a core system; unmet mandatory/go-live condition; absent control plan for evidenced serious exposure; misleading status risking a materially wrong decision; or blockage requiring Owner/Board of Supervisors decision; and
2. urgency or authority need, such as a binding milestone near/passed, increasing consequence, missing escalation, unresolved final accountability, failed handover, or no authorized decision.

Handling: report immediately; require one final accountable PIC, consequence, required decision, control action, and remediation deadline; monitor until controlled.

## CAM

Use for significant deviation or credible non-completion risk: overdue incomplete work, forecast after deadline, no revised forecast after delay, partial completion, repeated date changes, unresolved dependency, unclear final responsibility, source-status conflict, narrower scope, or no revised PIC commitment.

Handling: monitor weekly; require primary PIC, coordinating parties, missing outputs, remediation plan, and new date; escalate if no movement.

## VÀNG

Use when no significant deviation is established but verification is still needed: before deadline with unproven delivery capability, missing output evidence, unclear closure criteria, small scope mismatch, dependency with time remaining, activity-only updates, missing approval/acceptance/result, or unclear PIC boundary.

Handling: clarify PIC, responsibility boundary, output, and evidence; recheck next period; move to `CAM` if delay risk or unresolved ownership emerges.

## XANH

Requires positive evidence of either proportionate progress before deadline with a clear next milestone, or full in-scope completion with verifiable evidence and no material work/approval outstanding. PIC must be specific and primary versus coordinating roles clear.

Handling: monitor periodically while active; close only after verifying output, scope, evidence, and completion responsibility.

## XÁM

Use only when classification itself is unreliable: output cannot be determined, timing and recurring nature are unknown, progress is blank/non-informative, scope completed is unknowable, mixed outputs cannot be separated, PIC is absent/generic/contradictory, or directive cannot be compared with result.

Handling: require standardized PIC, objective, output, deadline, status, and evidence. Do not treat as controlled.

## Border rules

- `ĐỎ` vs `CAM`: choose `ĐỎ` only with evidenced serious consequence/mandatory failure plus immediate authority intervention; otherwise `CAM`.
- `CAM` vs `VÀNG`: `CAM` when deviation or credible delay risk exists; `VÀNG` when the issue is verification without significant deviation.
- `VÀNG` vs `XÁM`: `VÀNG` when assessable but incomplete; `XÁM` when the assessment itself is unreliable.
- `XANH` vs `VÀNG`: `XANH` only with positive evidence; otherwise verify under `VÀNG`.

## Snapshot rule

Reassess every snapshot. Keep the level only while current evidence supports it. Change it when consequence, readiness, deadline, progress, proof, PIC, dependency, or closure changes. Preserve history.
