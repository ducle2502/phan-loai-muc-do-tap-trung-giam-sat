# Classification rules

## Minimum facts

Assess objective/output, timing expectation, current result, evidence or testable completion claim, accountable PIC/unit, and material consequence/dependency/decision. Not every field must exist. Use `XÁM` only when gaps prevent reliable assessment.

## ĐỎ

Requires both:

1. evidenced serious consequence or failed mandatory readiness involving operational safety, law, liquidity, capital, critical data, or a core system; and
2. immediate authority need, such as unresolved mandatory/go-live condition, increasing consequence, absent control decision, or blockage requiring Chủ sở hữu/Ban Kiểm soát intervention.

PIC signals strengthen ĐỎ when there is no accountable owner, authority is inadequate, final accountability is unresolved, handover failed, or response is absent while consequence grows.

Handling: report immediately; require one final accountable PIC, consequence, required decision, control action, and remediation deadline; monitor until controlled.

## CAM

Use for significant deviation or credible non-completion risk: overdue incomplete work, forecast after deadline, no revised forecast after delay, partial completion, repeated date changes, unresolved dependency, unclear final responsibility, source-status conflict, narrower scope, or no revised PIC commitment.

Handling: monitor weekly; require primary PIC, coordinating parties, missing outputs, remediation plan, and new date; escalate if no movement.

## VÀNG

Use when no significant deviation is established but verification is needed: before deadline with unproven capability, missing output evidence, unclear closure criteria, small scope mismatch, dependency with time remaining, activity-only updates, missing approval/acceptance/result, or unclear PIC boundary.

Handling: clarify PIC, responsibility boundary, output, and evidence; recheck next period; move to `CAM` if delay risk or unresolved ownership emerges.

## XANH

Requires positive evidence of proportionate progress before deadline with a clear next milestone, or full in-scope completion with verifiable evidence and no material work/approval outstanding. PIC must be specific and primary versus coordinating roles clear.

Handling: monitor periodically while active; close only after verifying output, scope, evidence, and completion responsibility.

## XÁM

Use only when classification itself is unreliable: output cannot be determined, timing and recurring nature are unknown, progress is blank/non-informative, completed scope is unknowable, mixed outputs cannot be separated, PIC is absent/generic/contradictory, or directive cannot be compared with result.

Handling: require standardized PIC, objective, output, deadline, status, and evidence. Do not treat as controlled.

## Border rules

- `ĐỎ` vs `CAM`: choose `ĐỎ` only with serious consequence/mandatory failure plus immediate authority intervention; otherwise `CAM`.
- `CAM` vs `VÀNG`: choose `CAM` when deviation or credible delay risk exists; `VÀNG` when the issue is verification without significant deviation.
- `VÀNG` vs `XÁM`: choose `VÀNG` when assessable but incomplete; `XÁM` when assessment itself is unreliable.
- `XANH` vs `VÀNG`: choose `XANH` only with positive evidence; otherwise `VÀNG`.
